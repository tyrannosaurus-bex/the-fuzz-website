Care Instructions

⚠️ IMPORTANT!

When cleaning your items AVOID HEAT. NO HEAT! Always use COLD water and never use a hair blow dryer to dry your items – air dry with fans ONLY. Heat will damage your fur beyond fixing. DO NOT USE HEAT.

Quick Cleaning

Recommended after short meetups or photoshoots

  • Brush your items before wearing to fluff and soften the fur
  • Use pet wipes to wipe down all items, inside and out (avoiding the eyes on heads)
  • Brush and allow everything to dry
  • Feel free to use suit sprays or Febreze for extra fragrance

Body Suit Deep Clean

After every con or long day of suiting

  • Remove padding and feet if attached
  • Give suit a preliminary brush
  • Turn inside out and zip up
  • Wash on DELICATE with COLD WATER only
  • Turn right-side out and brush the fur
  • Air dry (no hangers! Use drying rack or chairs)
  • Use a fan to speed up drying
  • Ensure COMPLETELY DRY before storing

Tails, Paws & Arm Sleeves

Deep cleaning instructions

  • Give items a preliminary brush
  • Turn inside out (as best as possible)
  • Wash on DELICATE with COLD WATER only
  • Turn right-side in and brush, shaking out water
  • Hang arm sleeves to dry
  • Dry tails and paws flat on a towel
  • Brush/flip regularly to avoid kinking
  • Use a fan to speed up drying

Head & Feet Paws

Hand wash method

  • Give items a preliminary brush
  • Fill clean tub ¼ full with COLD water
  • Add ½ cup fabric safe detergent
  • Submerge and gently scrub (avoid eyes)
  • Focus on inside where bacteria builds up
  • Squeeze out soapy water
  • Rinse thoroughly with clean water
  • Brush the fur
  • Air dry with fan pointed inside
  • Brush regularly while drying

Storage Tips

Keep your items in great condition

  • Store in a dry, room temperature spot
  • Use a closet or plastic tote
  • Avoid hot attics
  • Avoid humid basements
  • Keep away from direct sunlight
  • Ensure items are completely dry before storing
